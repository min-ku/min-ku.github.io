## About Me

<p class="lede">Hi, I'm Minku Kim, a Ph.D. candidate in Robotics at <a href="https://oregonstate.edu/">Oregon State University</a> and a member of the <a href="https://mime.engineering.oregonstate.edu/research/drl/">Dynamic Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory</a>, advised by <a href="https://engineering.oregonstate.edu/people/alan-fern">Professor Alan Fern</a>.</p>

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering and Applied Mechanics from the University of Pennsylvania. In the [Dynamic Autonomy and Intelligent Robotics Lab](https://dair.seas.upenn.edu/), advised by [Professor Michael Posa](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/people/michael-posa/) and co-advised by [Professor Pratik Chaudhari](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/people/pratik-chaudhari/).

<p class="contact-note">I am always happy to discuss robotics research and potential collaborations. Feel free to <a href="mailto:kimminku@oregonstate.edu">get in touch</a>.</p>

## Research Interests

<ul class="interest-list">
  <li><strong>Legged &amp; Humanoid Robots:</strong> reinforcement learning, whole-body control, skill learning, and sim-to-real transfer</li>
  <li><strong>Robot Perception:</strong> 6D pose and shape estimation, multi-view vision, and sensor-rich feedback</li>
</ul>
